Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-02

Who uses these apps

These apps are personal-use tools. Only their owner signs in, with the owner's own Google account. They are not offered to other people, and no data about any other person is collected.

What data is accessed

With the owner's consent, the apps access files in the owner's Google Drive (for backup) and the owner's Google Sheets (for personal bookkeeping). Access is limited to what these two tasks need.

How the data is used

The data is used only to carry out the owner's own backup and bookkeeping. It is not sold and is not used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account page under "Third-party apps & services".
